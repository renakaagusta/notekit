Badges label state and metadata — sync status, encryption, conflicts. They're small,
pill-shaped, and mostly monochrome; color appears only for the two semantic meanings.

<BadgeShowcase />

## Tones

- **Neutral** / **Outline** — non-semantic metadata (tags, counts, labels).
- **Solid** — an active or selected state, using the neutral accent.
- **Success** / **Destructive** — the only colored tones, for synced vs. conflict states.

## Tokens

Height `22px`, [`full`](/foundations/radius) radius, `12px` semibold text. Colors read from
the [semantic tokens](/foundations/color); success and destructive are the sole hues.

## Guidance

- Keep the label to one or two words.
- Don't invent new colors for categories — use neutral tones and rely on the label.
