On mobile, NoteKit's primary navigation is a **left-slide drawer** rather than a bottom tab
bar. It's opened from the [app bar](/components/appbar) hamburger and holds the whole
navigation surface: the vault switcher, the six surfaces (Notes, Tasks, Links, Secrets,
Inbox, Settings), and the account footer. Tap the scrim or swipe left to dismiss.

<NavDrawerShowcase />

## Why a drawer, not tabs

Six top-level surfaces don't fit a comfortable bottom tab bar, and a drawer keeps the reading
and editing canvas full-width and undisturbed — the same reasoning Obsidian and Bear use. The
active surface is marked with the [`accent`](/foundations/color) fill.

## Tokens

Panel width `~78%`, [`popover`](/foundations/color) surface, a right `border` hairline, rows
at `14px` with [lucide](/foundations/icons) glyphs. The scrim is 50% black; the panel respects
the top and bottom safe-area insets.

## Guidance

- Desktop uses a persistent sidebar instead — same items, no scrim, always visible.
- Keep the drawer to navigation and account; per-item actions belong in a
  [menu](/components/menu), not here.
