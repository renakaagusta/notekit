Avatars identify a person — the account, a note's author, guests on a shared note. NoteKit
keeps them monochrome: when there's no image, a single initial on the neutral accent, never a
colored auto-generated background.

## Tokens

Circular ([`full`](/foundations/radius)), initial at 40% of the diameter, bold. The solid tone
uses [`primary`](/foundations/color); the muted tone uses `muted`. Stacked avatars add a
`2px` ring in the surface color to separate them.

## Guidance

- Prefer a real image (Gravatar) when available; fall back to a single initial, not two.
- Don't tint fallbacks by name — NoteKit avatars stay monochrome, like the rest of the system.
- Sizes: `24px` inline, `32px` in lists, `40px`+ in headers.
