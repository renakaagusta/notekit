---
title: Share a note
description: Share a single note with another person by email — end-to-end encrypted, with a safety-number check.
---

Sharing in NoteKit is **per item**: you grant one note (or task or link) to one person, not your whole
vault. The item is re-encrypted so their devices can read it; nothing else you have is exposed.

Sharing is done from the **app** (web or desktop). It relies on the recipient having published their
public keys, which happens automatically when they use NoteKit.

## Share an item

1. Open the note you want to share and choose **Share** (the lock icon).
2. Enter the recipient's **email address**. NoteKit looks them up in the key
   [directory](/how-it-works/sharing-and-revocation) and verifies their published device keys against
   their signing key.
3. Compare the **safety number** shown for that person, if you want out-of-band assurance you've got
   the right recipient.
4. Confirm. The note is re-encrypted to include the recipient's verified keys, and a grant is recorded
   in the vault.

The recipient doesn't need to accept anything — the shared item appears in their vault the next time
they sync.

## Stop sharing

Open the same Share dialog and remove the person. The item is re-encrypted to the reduced set, so they
can't read **future** changes.

<Callout type="warn">
Revocation is **forward-only**. Removing someone stops future access, but Git history still contains the
versions they could already read — you can't un-send what was already shared. If the content is
sensitive, rotate the underlying secret rather than relying on hiding history. See
[sharing & revocation](/how-it-works/sharing-and-revocation#forward-only-revocation).
</Callout>

<Callout type="info">
Sharing is currently available in the app UI. The share grant and the recipient email are visible to
the server as metadata; the note **contents** stay encrypted.
</Callout>
