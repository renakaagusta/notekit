Toasts confirm that something happened — a note saved, a vault synced — without interrupting.
They appear briefly, dismiss themselves, and never demand action.

<ToastShowcase />

## Tokens

Filled with the [`primary`](/foundations/color) surface, radius
[`md`](/foundations/radius), `medium` [shadow](/foundations/elevation), a
[lucide](/foundations/icons) status icon, and a `~2.4s` auto-dismiss.

## Placement

- **Desktop** — bottom-right, stacking upward.
- **Mobile** — bottom-center, above the safe-area inset and any pinned toolbar, so it never
  hides behind the home indicator.

## Guidance

- Toasts are for confirmations, not errors that need a decision — use an inline message or a
  [dialog](/components/dialog) for those.
- One line. No buttons except a single optional Undo.
