## Families

- **UI + editor** — `Inter`. The product runs Inter for both chrome and the editor body.
- **Monospace** — `JetBrains Mono`. Code, terminals, secrets, and keys.
- **Marketing** — `DM Sans`. The landing surface keeps a distinct display font. This is a
  deliberate two-font decision (product vs marketing), not drift.

## Scale

The product leans on two anchor sizes: chrome at ~13.5px (`ui`) and editor body at 16px
(`editor`, with generous 1.7 leading for sustained reading). The rest of the scale supports
labels, captions, and titles.

## Weights

NoteKit uses a slightly tightened weight ramp: regular 400, medium 510, semibold 590, bold
680. The non-round values track Inter's optical weights for crisper chrome.
