---
title: Motion
description: Durations and easing curves for transitions.
---

Motion in NoteKit is quick and unshowy — feedback, not spectacle. Most transitions land
between 100ms and 250ms.

## Durations

- **`instant` (100ms)** — hover and press feedback.
- **`fast` (150ms)** — small state changes, toggles.
- **`regular` (250ms)** — panels, drawers, most UI transitions.
- **`slow` (400ms)** — the largest layout shifts only.

## Easing

- **`out`** — the default for things entering or settling; decelerates into place.
- **`in`** — for things leaving.
- **`in-out`** — for movement that starts and ends on screen.
- **`out-expo`** — snappy, overshoot-free exits for larger surfaces.

## Usage

Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: when a user opts out, transitions collapse to near-zero
duration rather than animating. Never gate meaning behind motion.
