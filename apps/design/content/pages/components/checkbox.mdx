title: Checkbox

Checkboxes are for selecting items and toggling form options where the change applies on
submit. They're small (16px) with a `sm` radius and fill with the neutral accent when checked.
Try them:

<CheckboxShowcase />

## Tokens

Box `16px`, radius [`sm` (5px)](/foundations/radius), checked fill from
[`primary`](/foundations/color), check glyph from [lucide](/foundations/icons).

## Checkbox vs. switch

- **Checkbox** — selection, or a setting that takes effect on save. Often appears in groups.
- **[Switch](/components/switch)** — a single setting that takes effect immediately.
