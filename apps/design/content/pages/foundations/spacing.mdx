Spacing is a small, predictable scale. The product's chrome is dense — most gaps land at
`2` (8px) or `3` (12px). Larger steps structure sections and page padding.

<ScaleTable category="space" />

## Usage

- Prefer scale steps over arbitrary pixel values. If a gap needs a value not on the scale,
  that's a signal to question the layout, not to add a one-off.
- `1`–`3` handle inline and component-internal spacing; `4`–`6` handle section and page
  rhythm.
