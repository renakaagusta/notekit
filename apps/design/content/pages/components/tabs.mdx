Tabs switch between sibling views that share a context — Notes, Tasks, Links for a project.
They're a compact segmented control; the active segment lifts onto the background surface.

<TabsShowcase />

## Tokens

Track uses the [`muted`](/foundations/color) surface with an inner `background`-filled active
segment, radius [`md`](/foundations/radius), label at [`small`/`13px`](/foundations/typography)
semibold.

## On mobile

Keep the same segmented control, but let the track **scroll horizontally** when the labels
don't fit rather than wrapping or truncating — the active tab always scrolls into view.

## Guidance

- Tabs are for peer views, not steps in a flow and not navigation between unrelated pages.
- Keep labels to one word where possible; three to five tabs is the comfortable range.
