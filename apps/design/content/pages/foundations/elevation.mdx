Elevation is used sparingly — the product is mostly flat, with shadow reserved for
genuinely floating surfaces. Three steps cover the range.

<ShadowGrid />

## Usage

- **`low`** — resting cards and subtle lift.
- **`medium`** — popovers and dropdowns.
- **`high`** — dialogs and the command menu, the things that sit above everything.

On the dark product theme, elevation reads more from the `surface` → `surface-2` stack and
borders than from shadow. Do not stack shadows to fake depth — reach for a higher surface
token instead.
