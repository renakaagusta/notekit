NoteKit is **end-to-end encrypted**. Content is encrypted on your device before it leaves it, and
only your devices hold the keys to read it back.

<Callout>
This page is the plain-language overview. For the full technical design — algorithms, key
derivation, the device roster, envelope encryption, and the threat model — see
[Security & cryptography](/security).
</Callout>

## Your identity and recovery phrase

When you first sign in, NoteKit generates an encryption identity for you and a **24-word recovery
phrase**. That phrase is the master key to your vault. NoteKit never sees it and cannot recover it
for you.

## Devices

Each device (web install, desktop, mobile, CLI) has its own key, stored locally on that device.
You add a new device by pairing it from an existing one — no need to re-type the recovery phrase.
If you lose every device, the recovery phrase is how you get back in.

<Callout type="warn">
Clearing your browser storage or reinstalling the app removes that device's local key. Recover
with the 24-word phrase or from another device that is still paired.
</Callout>

## Agents and least privilege

Agents get their own key. By default an agent can **write** to your vault but cannot **read**
existing encrypted content unless you explicitly share it with the agent's key. An agent never
holds your master phrase. See [MCP](/mcp) for how agents connect.
