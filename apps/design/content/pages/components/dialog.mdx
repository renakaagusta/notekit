Dialogs interrupt for a single focused decision — a confirm, a destructive action, a short
form. They sit on a dimmed overlay with the highest [elevation](/foundations/elevation). Open
it:

<DialogShowcase />

## Anatomy

- **Overlay** — dims the page and closes the dialog on click-outside.
- **Panel** — a `popover`-token surface with `lg` radius and the `high` shadow.
- **Title + body** — one clear question, one short line of context.
- **Actions** — right-aligned; the primary or destructive action last.

## Tokens

Panel background from [`popover`](/foundations/color), radius
[`lg` (8px)](/foundations/radius), [`high`](/foundations/elevation) shadow, overlay at 50%
black.

## Guidance

- One decision per dialog. If it grows into a multi-step flow, it's a page, not a dialog.
- Always offer a non-destructive way out (Cancel, click-outside, Escape).
- Name the action on the button — "Delete", not "OK".
