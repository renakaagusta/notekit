Buttons are compact (32px tall) and monochrome. The **primary** button is the only filled,
high-emphasis action per view; everything else steps down to secondary, ghost, or — for
irreversible actions — destructive.

<ButtonShowcase />

## Variants

- **Primary** — the one main action. Filled with the neutral accent (near-white on dark,
  near-black on light).
- **Secondary** — supporting actions; a bordered, low-contrast surface.
- **Ghost** — tertiary actions and toolbar buttons; no background until hovered.
- **Destructive** — deletes and other irreversible actions only.

## Tokens

Height `32px`, radius [`md` (6px)](/foundations/radius), label at
[`small`/`13px`](/foundations/typography) semibold. Colors read from the semantic
[`primary` / `secondary`](/foundations/color) tokens, so buttons re-theme with the palette.

## Guidance

- One primary per view. If two actions feel equally important, one of them isn't.
- Lead with a [lucide](/foundations/icons) icon only when it clarifies the action.
