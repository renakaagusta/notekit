---
title: Tooltip
description: A short label revealed on hover or focus.
---

Tooltips give a short, non-essential label to an icon-only control — what a button does, when
something last synced. They appear on hover and on keyboard focus, and never contain essential
information or actions.

<TooltipShowcase />

## Tokens

Filled with the [`primary`](/foundations/color) surface, radius
[`md`](/foundations/radius), `12px` text, `medium` [shadow](/foundations/elevation).

## On mobile — don't rely on hover

Touch has no hover, so a tooltip must never carry information the user needs. On mobile,
either the control is self-explanatory, the label is shown inline, or the detail moves into a
[bottom sheet](/components/dialog) on tap. Treat tooltips as a desktop-only enhancement.

## Guidance

- Never put essential text or the only copy of an action in a tooltip.
- Keep it to a few words; if it needs a sentence, it's not a tooltip.
