A card groups related content on a raised surface — a note in a grid, a settings panel, a
stat. It's the quietest container in the system: a single hairline border on the `card`
surface, no shadow at rest. Structure is optional — an unadorned body, or a header and a
footer divided by hairlines.

<CardShowcase />

## Anatomy

- **Surface** — the `card` background with a `border` hairline; use it, not a shadow, to
  separate a card from the page.
- **Header / footer** (optional) — divided from the body by a `border` hairline; good for a
  title + actions, or metadata like a folder and timestamp.
- **Body** — the content itself, at comfortable padding.

## Tokens

Background from [`card`](/foundations/color), `1px` [`border`](/foundations/color),
[`lg` (8px)](/foundations/radius) radius, [`4`/16px](/foundations/spacing) padding. Elevation
stays flat at rest — reach for [`low`](/foundations/elevation) only when a card is genuinely
lifted (dragged, floating).

## Guidance

- Prefer a border over a shadow to delineate a card — the product is mostly flat.
- Keep one idea per card; if it needs tabs or many sections, it's a page.
- A whole card may be a link (a note preview) — give it a visible focus ring and keep inner
  actions reachable.
