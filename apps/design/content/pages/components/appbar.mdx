The app bar anchors the top of a surface: where you are, and the actions for what you're
looking at. On desktop it leads with a breadcrumb and trails with icon actions.

<AppBarShowcase />

## Tokens

Height `48px`, a `border` hairline, [`ghost`](/components/button)-style icon buttons at
`30px`. Breadcrumb text at [`small`/`13px`](/foundations/typography); the current item is
full-contrast, ancestors are muted.

## On mobile

Phones don't have room for a breadcrumb, and the primary job becomes **navigation**. The
mobile app bar leads with a hamburger that opens the [navigation drawer](/components/navigation-drawer),
centers the surface title, and keeps a single primary action on the right. It respects the
top safe-area inset so it never sits under the status bar.

<AppBarMobileShowcase />

## Guidance

- One primary action in the bar; everything else goes in a [menu](/components/menu).
- Keep the title to the current surface or note — don't restate the app name.
- NoteKit's mobile shell has no bottom tab bar; navigation lives in the drawer behind the
  hamburger, and the editor's formatting toolbar pins to the bottom safe-area instead.
